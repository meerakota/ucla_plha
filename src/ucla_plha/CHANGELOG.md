Unreleased
* This release covers California: the UCERF3 source models (ucerf3_fm31, ucerf3_fm32, and their gridded seismicity) and the four NGA-West2 ground motion models (ask14, bssa14, cb14, cy14) implemented in ucla_plha. It does not depend on pygmm.
* ASK14: the magnitude taper T2 of the hanging wall term was zero for M >= 5.5, which removed the hanging wall effect of all events larger than M5.5 (Equation 12 sets T2 = 0 for M <= 5.5); T3 is now restricted to hanging wall sites, so vertical ruptures with Rx < 0 no longer give NaN.
* CB14: R1 was computed as (zbor - ztor) * radians(dip) instead of W cos(dip) = (zbor - ztor) / tan(dip) (Equation 11).
* ASK14 with a user-specified z1p0: Sa1180 no longer includes a basin term computed from the site's z1p0, and the a43 to a46 soil depth coefficients are interpolated linearly between the Vs30 bin centers (150, 250, 400, and 700 m/s), following the application guidelines of Abrahamson et al. (2014), instead of the steps of Equation 17. PGA changes by up to 0.13 ln units for soft sites with deep basins. ASK14, BSSA14, CB14, and CY14 now match pygmm (an independent implementation; tests/data/gmm_reference.json) for 432 scenarios with default basin depths, and ASK14 also with user-specified z1p0.
* get_Rx_Rx1_Ry0: the sign of Rx was set by comparing |Rx| with |Rx1|, which made sites directly above a dipping rupture footwall sites. The sign now depends on which side of the top edge the site is on, and Rx1 = Rx - W cos(dip).
* UCERF3 conversion (utilities/convert_ucerf3_fault_source_models.py, convert_ucerf3_point_source_models.py): the down-dip edges of west-dipping faults were projected east, affecting Rjb, Rrup, Rx, Rx1, and Ry0; rupture ztor, zbor, and dip were computed from mis-indexed arrays (and zbor used the minimum instead of the maximum); the reverse and normal rates of the gridded seismicity had swapped style labels; and the area-weighted average dip of vertical ruptures was 90.00000000000001 from round-off, so ASK14 treated them as dipping (now clamped at 90). The UCERF3 source model files are regenerated.
* The ground motion models and geometry use explicit float64 arrays.
* ucerf3_fm31 and ucerf3_fm32 fault geometry and rupture depths now follow UCERF3 as implemented in OpenSHA and the USGS NSHM. (1) Aseismicity: the conversion used the full subsection planes from the trace to the lower depth, although UCERF3 reduces each subsection's area by its aseismic slip factor (0.1 for most faults, up to 0.9 for creeping faults) and its rupture areas and magnitudes are those of the reduced planes (OpenSHA FaultSection.getReducedAveUpperDepth, StirlingGriddedSurface; FaultSystemRupSet.getSurfaceForRupture with aseisReducesArea, the BaseFaultSystemSolutionERF default; nshmp-lib and the nshm-conus UCERF3 ruptures.csv). The upper edge of each subsection now moves down-dip from the trace to upper depth + aseismic slip factor * (lower depth - upper depth) and the lower edge does not move: dipping subsections lose up to 4.9 km (San Pedro Escarpment) of their up-dip part, and the rRup of controlling dipping subsections increases by up to about 1.5 km and agrees with the USGS 2018 NSHM disaggregation (e.g. Ventura - Pitas Point 1.07 to 2.63 km, USGS 2.65; Elysian Park 3.84 to 5.38 km, USGS 5.40). (2) Rupture ztor and zbor are now the averages of the reduced subsection upper and lower depths weighted by the reduced subsection areas, as OpenSHA (CompoundSurface.getAveRupTopDepth, getAveRupBottomDepth) and the nshm-conus ruptures.csv, instead of the shallowest upper depth and deepest lower depth; the rupture dip is weighted by the reduced areas. Compared with the USGS 2018 NSHM FaultSystem hazard (UCERF3, PGA, Vs30 760 m/s, nshmp ground motion models, rates scaled by the 0.97 of the nshm-conus ruptures.csv), the 475- and 2475-year PGA differences go from -4.4%/-3.0% to -0.3%/+0.0% at Moss Landing, +0.4%/+2.8% to +0.3%/+1.1% in Los Angeles, -2.4%/-1.5% to -0.2%/-0.1% in the Bay Area, and +1.1%/+3.0% to +0.1%/+1.0% in Ventura. The example configurations (Moss Landing) change by -0.04% (BSSA14 only) to +4.3% in 475- and 2475-year PGA (+1.4% to +2.0% for the four NGA-West2 models) and -1.6% to -2.5% in FSL. utilities/convert_ucerf3_fault_source_models.py reproduces the earlier files with UCERF3_APPLY_ASEISMICITY=0 UCERF3_RUPTURE_DEPTHS=shallowest.
* Rx and Rx1 of fault ruptures with several fault segments are now those of the segment with the smallest Rrup, as in nshmp-lib (SystemRuptureSet), instead of the minimum over the segments, which, because Rx is signed, was the most footwall-side segment and often turned hanging-wall sites into footwall sites. The earlier convention is available with "constraints": {"rupture_rx": "minimum"}.
* Fault source Rx and Ry0 now come from the GC2 coordinates of Spudich and Chiou (2015, USGS Open-File Report 2015-1028), as in the NGA-West2 and NGA-West3 flatfiles, for all fault source models ("constraints": {"rupture_rx": "gc2"}, the new default): the upper edges of the pieces of all segments of a rupture are its trace (consecutive pieces more than 1 km apart start a new trace, as for multi-fault ruptures), a segment discordant with the nominal strike is reversed as a whole, Rx = T, Ry0 is the distance beyond the ends of the U range, and Rx1 = Rx minus the horizontal width of the segment with the smallest Rrup. Before, Rx was that of the closest segment (nshmp-lib) and Ry0 the minimum over the segments; get_Rx_Rx1_Ry0 gives Ry0 = 0 for vertical rectangles wherever the site is (the down-dip edge has no length), so every rupture with a vertical segment had Ry0 = 0. The site-independent part of GC2 (orientation, discordance, offsets along strike, U max of every rupture) is computed once per source model and kept for the session (geometry.gc2_rupture_setup; about 7 s for UCERF3), and the site-dependent part sums the weights of the pieces per segment (geometry.gc2_segment_sums, gc2_rupture_coordinates): about 0.8 s per site for UCERF3. Each segment is oriented so that the fault dips to the right of its trace. "rupture_rx": "closest_section" or "minimum" give the earlier conventions, and geometry.gc2_coordinates computes GC2 for any planar traces. South Napa (nc72282711): Rx and Ry0 within 0.1 km of the NGA-West3 flatfile within 15 km and within 0.5% of the distance at 30 to 160 km.
* The rJB and rRup of the fault triangles are now computed in a projection on a sphere centred on the site (azimuthal equidistant, R = 6371.0072 km; geometry.local_triangle_distance; "constraints": {"triangle_frame": "local"}, the new default), so they are measured along the Earth's surface and rRup >= rJB. The Earth-centred Cartesian distances ("triangle_frame": "geocentric") are chords and, because the vertical at the fault tilts toward a distant site, give rRup < rJB for most ruptures beyond about 100 km (and for 71% of the UCERF3 ruptures within 100 km of Los Angeles, which reach the surface); they differ from the local ones by about 0.03 km (rJB) and 0.2 to 0.3 km (rRup) at 200 to 500 km. The local distances reproduce the NGA-West3 flatfile rJB and rRup of South Napa (nc72282711) within 0.05 km at 3 to 156 km (tests/test_gc2_local_projection.py); the NGA-West3 database uses surface distances on a sphere of about 6371 km.
* Hazard changes from the GC2 Rx/Ry0 and the site-centred triangle distances with the four NGA-West2 models (0.25 each): UCERF3 FM3.1 and FM3.2 (example 1 models) at Moss Landing, Los Angeles, Ventura, and the Bay Area -0.09% to +0.09% in 475- and 2475-year PGA (nearly all from GC2).
* cetin_et_al_2018: optional input parameter uncertainty of Cetin et al. (2018b, MethodsX 5, 1556-1575; Eqs. 4 and 5). It is off by default ("parameter_uncertainty": false, the earlier behavior); with "parameter_uncertainty": true, "sigma_n160", "sigma_fc", "cov_sigmavp", and "cov_csr" (default 0) are combined with the model uncertainty as sigma_tot^2 = 0.392 sigma_input^2 + 2.95^2.
* Disaggregation options: "disaggregation": {"distance_metric": "rrup"} bins the ruptures by rRup, as the USGS NSHM disaggregation (nshmp-lib Disaggregator) does for every source type (the default, "default", is unchanged: rJB if a ground motion model uses rJB, otherwise rRup; "rjb" is also available), and, for PSHA, {"means": true} adds "disaggregation_means" (binned contribution and mean magnitude, distance, and epsilon, in total and, with "source_model_hazard", per source model). The default disaggregation output is unchanged.
* get_disagg: events outside the bins (below the first or at or above the last edge of the magnitude, distance, or epsilon bins) are no longer binned. They used to be added to a neighboring bin (above the last edge) or to give a wrong bin or an error (below the first edge), e.g. epsilon above the last edge of 3 went to the first epsilon bin of the next distance bin. The bins now sum to less than 100% if events fall outside them (the "residual" of nshmp-lib).
* "constraints": {"truncation_level": n} truncates the ground motion distribution at n standard deviations above the median and renormalizes it, as the nshmp-lib TRUNCATION_UPPER_ONLY exceedance model (the USGS NSHM uses 3; default: no truncation). "output": {"psha": {"source_model_hazard": true}} adds the weighted hazard curve of each source model to the output.
